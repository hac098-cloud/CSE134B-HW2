The Feeling of Shooting

Physical Contact and Conditioning
I love basketball because it involves a lot of physical contact. Since I am naturally lean and small, this motivates me to work out and build a stronger physique. Defeating opponents who are physically stronger than me brings me joy and a great sense of accomplishment. Making a basket makes me feel happy.

Friendship and Teamwork
I believe playing basketball fosters friendships while also providing a good workout. I particularly enjoy teaming up with friends to defeat stronger opponents.

My Goal
My goal is to dunk. I think dunking looks really cool, so I train my legs three times a week, hoping to achieve this goal one day.
